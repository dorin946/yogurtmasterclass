Module 6, Guest Expert

Preservatives and Stabilizers. The commercial side of yogurt production.

If you eventually want to move beyond fresh daily sales and supply businesses such as shops, restaurants, or other customers, you will need to understand how shelf life, preservation, and product stability work.

This module is taught by a food science expert who explains what preservatives and stabilizers do, how they are used, and the considerations involved in commercial dairy production. Dorinda does not personally use preservatives in her own fresh daily batches. This module exists for students who want to understand the commercial side.

Potassium Sorbate and Sodium Benzoate

The two most common preservatives for yogurt. When to use them, how much to use, and how to mix them safely.

Potassium Sorbate and Natamycin

Natamycin is a natural antifungal used in commercial dairy. This guide covers how to combine it with Potassium Sorbate for extended shelf life.

Yogurt Stabilizers

Stabilizers stop your yogurt from separating and going watery during storage or transport. Learn which ones work for drinking yogurt and which work for Greek yogurt.

You also get the full PDF guides for all three. Real measurements, real instructions from a food science expert. Included with your class.
